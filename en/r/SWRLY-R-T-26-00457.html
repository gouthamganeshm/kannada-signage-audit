South Western Railway sends an inspection that found English-only boards at Dharwad station

A 34-page reply, mostly in Hindi: two covering letters, Railway Board circulars and a Department of Official Language letter (Annexure I), and official-language inspection reports for Dharwad, Vasco-da-Gama and Solur stations and the Hubballi carriage workshop, with compliance letters from field offices (Annexure II).

What was asked, and what came back

1Circulars issued under the OM“Please See Annexure - I”: Railway Board letters of 10.01.2024, 03.02.2026, 21.05.2026 (forwarding the minutes of the 62nd Railway Hindi Advisory Committee meeting), 18.06.2026 and 14.07.2026, and the Department of Official Language's D.O. letter of 04.12.2023
2Action taken to use Kannada“Please See Annexure - II” (one cell covers points 2, 3 and 4): inspection reports and compliance letters from offices
3Inspection / compliance reportsSame answer as point 2; inspections of Dharwad (27.06.2026), Vasco-da-Gama (21.03.2026) and Solur (28.01.2026) stations, the Hubballi carriage workshop (12.02.2026) and the Construction Organisation office (21.01.2025)
4Corrective action on non-complianceSame answer as point 2; the Dharwad and Vasco-da-Gama reports ask for the deficiencies to be rectified and a compliance report sent; no action-taken report is enclosed
5Officer responsible for monitoring“Rajbhasha Dept., HQ/ SWR & its subordinate offices”

Why it matters

The Dharwad station inspection of 27.06.2026 photographs five English-only boards, including ‘Electrical Store Room’, ‘Toilet’ and the ground floor plan, and proposes Kannada-Hindi-English text for each; other public boards were found trilingual. Page 4 of that 10-page report is not in the reply. The corrections proposed after the Vasco-da-Gama (Goa) inspection are in Hindi only. The Solur station and Construction Organisation inspection reports, and letters from the Central Workshop in Mysuru and the Construction Organisation in Bengaluru, say boards are trilingual or carry Kannada first; none of these has photographs. The Department of Official Language's letter of 04.12.2023 says the ministry's consolidated compliance report had ‘not yet’ been received; the Railway Board had asked for a report within 10 days on 10.01.2024. Both covering letters have a Hindi and English letterhead only.
