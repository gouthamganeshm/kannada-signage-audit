Claims compliance, no proof

Mysuru Central Workshop says it received no circular but “strictly” follows a trilingual policy; it mentions inspections but sends no report

A two-page letter in table form. No document or photograph.

𝕏WhatsApp↓ Image
Public authoritySouth Western Railway, Central Workshop, Mysore South
Registration no.SWRMW/R/X/26/00013
Filed01 Sep 2026
Replied28 Sep 2026 day 27
Received viaRTI Online portal

What was asked, and what came back

1Circulars issued under the OM“No separate circulars, office orders, instructions and guidelines received at this office. However, regional language in being used in all public information displays, boards and nameplates in this unit.”
2Action taken to use Kannada“Trilingual/bilingual display policy (Kannada, Hindi and English) is strictly followed across all public information displays, boards, nameplates, signages, etc. in this unit.” No photograph
3Inspection / compliance reports“Regular reviews and inspections regarding official language implementation including regional language displays on public signages are conducted during routine inspection cycles by Rajbhasha implementation Committee.” No report is supplied
4Corrective action on non-compliance“No such information available in this office, since the instructions are being complied.”
5Officer responsible for monitoring“Officer - Dy Mukhya Rajbhasha Adhikari /MYSS. Department- Rajbhasha Department.”

Why it matters

The workshop says it received no separate circular on the OM. Point 3 asked for copies of inspection reports; the reply says inspections take place but supplies no report and gives no date. The answer to point 2 describes the policy as “Trilingual/bilingual”. The letterhead is in Hindi and English only.
