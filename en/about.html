Method

Each application asked the same five questions about OM No. 14013/04/2022: the circulars issued under it, the action taken to use Kannada on boards, inspection reports, corrective action where boards did not comply, and the officer responsible for monitoring.

Replies came through the RTI Online portal and by e-mail. What is published here is the original document as received. The only changes are that the applicant's home address, PIN code, personal e-mail and any mobile number have been permanently removed, and the applicant's seal has been added. A person read every page before it was published.

Officials' names, designations, office addresses and office contact details are public information under the RTI Act and are left as issued.
