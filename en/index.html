Campaign report

Do Central Government offices in Karnataka put Kannada on their boards?

In 2022 the Department of Official Language told every Central office in a non-Hindi state to use the regional language on public boards and nameplates. This audit asked Central Government offices in Karnataka, under the RTI Act, to show what they did about it. Every reply is published here, as received.
