Method

About this campaign

A citizen's audit, under the Right to Information Act, of whether Central Government offices in Karnataka use Kannada on their boards and nameplates.

The campaign

In June 2022 the Department of Official Language, Ministry of Home Affairs, issued Office Memorandum No. 14013/04/2022. It told every ministry and department that Central Government offices in non-Hindi-speaking states were not using the regional language on public boards and nameplates as the rules require, and asked them to report how their offices were complying.

Starting in July 2026, this campaign filed RTI applications with Central Government ministries and departments, railway zones and divisions, banks, insurers, public-sector companies, tribunals and other Central offices in Karnataka, asking what they had done about that order: the circulars they issued, the action they took, the inspections they ran, the corrective steps they took, and who is responsible for monitoring it.

The replies are published here one at a time, as received, after each one has been read and checked. Each reply gets a short summary in Kannada and English and its own illustration. Documents in Hindi are shown with an English machine translation beside them. The campaign was announced on X and will close with a summary of what the replies show.

What the order says

Issued on 21.06.2022 and signed by Joint Secretary Dr. Meenakshi Jolly, the OM recalls a 1977 order (OM No. 1/14013/5/76-OL(A-1), 18.06.1977) that asked Central offices in 'B' and 'C' region states to use the regional language, Hindi and English, in that order, on boards and nameplates, an instruction repeated in 2010, 2011 and 2017. It notes that this is not being followed and that the issue was raised in Parliament, and asks every ministry and department to report, by 09.07.2022, the compliance status of all its offices in non-Hindi states, including attached and subordinate offices, companies it controls, nationalised banks and public-sector undertakings.

14013/04/2022-National Policy (Policy) Government of India Home Ministry Department of Official Language

4th Floor, NDCC-2 Building, Jai Singh Road, New Delhi - 01 Date 21.06.2022

Office memorandum

Subject: Regarding providing information about the use of regional languages in the boards, name plates etc. installed by the Central Government offices located in non-Hindi speaking areas for public information.

It has come to the notice of the Department of Official Language that regional languages are not being used as per the rules in the boards, name plates etc. installed by the Central Government offices located in non-Hindi speaking areas for public information. This matter has also been raised in the Parliament by the Honorable MP.

2. In this regard, refer to the Office Memorandum No. 1/14013/5/76-RHA(A-1) dated 18.06.1977 issued by the Department of Official Language regarding the above mentioned subject (copy attached). Through this office memorandum, guidelines were issued regarding the use of regional language, Hindi and English language respectively in boards, name plates etc. in the central government offices in non-Hindi speaking states i.e. 'B' and 'C' regions. These guidelines are updated from time to time vide Office Memorandum No. 1/14013/03/2010-R.A.(Policy-1) dated 01.07.2010, O.M. No. 14013/07/2010-R.A.(Policy) dated 07.04.2011 and O.M. No. 14013/07/2010-National Policy (Policy) dated 28.04.2017.

3. Therefore, all the Ministries/Departments of the Government of India are requested to inform the Department of Official Language by 09.07.2022 about the status of compliance of these guidelines by all their offices (including attached/subordinate offices/controlled/owned companies, nationalized banks, public sector undertakings etc.) located in non-Hindi speaking states.

Attachment: As per

(Dr. Meenakshi Jolly) Joint Secretary, Government of India Tel: 011-23438130

Joint Secretaries (Admn.) of all Ministries/Departments of the Government of India

The earlier orders, in English

The order of 18.06.1977 (OM No. 1/14013/5/76-OL(A-1)) that the 2022 OM recalls, and the order of 07.04.2011 (OM No. 1/14013/07/2010-O.L.(Policy-1)) that keeps it in force for Regions B and C, with their English text.

Method

Each application asked the same five questions about OM No. 14013/04/2022: the circulars issued under it, the action taken to use Kannada on boards, inspection reports, corrective action where boards did not comply, and the officer responsible for monitoring.

Officials' names, designations, office addresses and office contact details are public information under the RTI Act and are left as issued.

Every document here was received under the RTI Act, 2005, in reply to applications about Department of Official Language OM No. 14013/04/2022 (21.06.2022) on regional languages in public signage. The applicant's personal details are redacted; officials' names and designations are left as issued.
